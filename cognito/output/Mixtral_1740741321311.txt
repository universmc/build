Beneath the vast celestial sea,
Twelve constellations dance in glee.
A cosmic ballet, an eternal round,
In the heavens, they are tightly bound.

Aries, the Ram, bold and brash,
First to leap, a daring dash.
Taurus, the Bull, strong and sturdy,
Earthbound, yet with the stars concurdy.

Gemini, the Twins, ever playful,
In the sky, their spirits are revelous.
Cancer, the Crab, with a gentle touch,
In the stars, they cling so much.

Leo, the Lion, regal and proud,
Roaring loud, they rule the crowd.
Virgo, the Maiden, pure and bright,
Guides the stars in the darkest night.

Libra, the Scales, seeking balance,
In the cosmos, they find their alliance.
Scorpio, the Scorpion, with a sting,
In the heavens, their power does sing.

Sagittarius, the Archer, ever free,
Shoots for the stars, as easy as can be.
Capricorn, the Goat, with ambition grand,
In the universe, they make their stand.

Aquarius, the Water Bearer, pouring love,
From the stars, it flows like a dove.
Pisces, the Fish, swimming in dreams,
In the cosmos, they forever gleam.

Twelve constellations, an endless dance,
In the night sky, they take their chance.
A celestial symphony, a grand design,
In the universe, they forever shine.
