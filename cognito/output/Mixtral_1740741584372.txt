Beneath the vast celestial dome,
The zodiac's twelve constellations roam.
A mystic dance of stars unfolds,
A tale of ancient myths and legends, bold.

In twinkling lights, Aries leaps,
The ram with courage that never sleeps.
His golden fleece, a prize so rare,
A symbol of strength, beyond compare.

Taurus, the bull, steadfast and true,
His charge is fierce, his heart is new.
With stars as horns, he stands tall,
A guardian of the sky, protecting us all.

Gemini's twin stars shine bright,
Two souls entwined in eternal light.
Their stories whispered on the wind,
A dance of knowledge, without an end.

Cancer, the crab, embraces the sea,
Its claws hold tight to memories.
A shield of stars, a maternal grace,
Nurturing life in the vast empty space.

Leo, the lion, roars with pride,
A king of beasts, by starlight guided.
His mane ablaze, a majestic sight,
A symbol of power, in the endless night.

Virgo, the maiden, pure and kind,
Harvest's bounty, in her hands we find.
Her stars a guide, in the darkest hour,
A gentle touch, a soothing power.

Libra's scales, in perfect balance,
Seek harmony in the cosmic dance.
Justice and truth, in every move,
A delicate dance, for the stars above.

Scorpio, the scorpion, stings with might,
A warrior's spirit, burning bright.
A guardian of secrets, in the depths of space,
Revealing truths, in the hidden place.

Sagittarius, the archer, aims for the sky,
With wisdom and truth, his arrows fly.
A quest for knowledge, his eternal goal,
A journey through the stars, his soul.

Capricorn, the goat, climbs so high,
A leader with ambition, reaching for the sky.
His stars a path, in the darkest night,
A beacon of hope, in the endless flight.

Aquarius, the water bearer, pours for all,
A visionary's dream, for one and all.
With stars as chalice, in the cosmic sea,
A future bright, for you and me.

Pisces, the fish, swim in harmony,
Their dreams and visions, a celestial symphony.
A dance of intuition, in the starry sea,
A journey through the zodiac, forever free.
